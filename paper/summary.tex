\noindent{\Large\textbf{The Hidden Options Market}}\\[0.2em]
{\large Box lending, fund packages and dealer gamma in OCC's FLEX report}\\[0.3em]
\small One-page summary of a working paper and its code:
\url{https://github.com/ITheClixs/hidden-options-market}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{The question.} FLEX options are exchange options with negotiated
terms. OCC clears them and reports every position daily, but they are missing from the
listed chains that option research and every gamma-exposure (GEX) feed use. What is in
this hidden book, and does its risk matter?

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Data.} OCC's daily FLEX report, \PanelStart{} to \PanelEnd{}
(\PanelDates{} dates); Cboe's FLEX trade reports (\BoxRExecutions{} executions); issuer
holdings files; the Treasury curve and SOFR; delayed listed chains. All free and public.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{1. Mostly lending and stock replacement.} Box spreads, deep calls
through which funds hold their underlying, and same-strike pairs hold
\AnatFinancingValueShare{} of mark value, \AnatNonOptionOIShare{} of open interest and
\AnatNonOptionGamma{} of gross gamma. (A pair is a forward only if held on opposite
sides, which the report cannot show.)

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{2. Box lending, priced from trades.} \BoxRTradedFace{} of box face is
confirmed by traded orders (counts alone suggest \AnatBoxFace{}).
\BoxRHeadlineTrades{} boxes of up to a year traded at a median \BoxRSpreadMedian{}bp
over Treasuries (95\% interval \BoxRMedianLow{}--\BoxRMedianHigh{}bp), rising from
\BoxRYearTwentyTwentyFive{}bp in 2025 to \BoxRYearTwentyTwentySix{}bp in 2026, and
\BoxRSofrMedian{}bp over SOFR. OCC's marks value the same boxes at about the Treasury
rate. One ETF holds \HoldBoxShareOfItsBox{} of the largest box.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{3. Holdings-signed dealer gamma against the GEX convention.} For one
issuer's \HoldFlexFunds{} defined-outcome funds, the counterparties are net long
\HoldGammaNet{} of gamma per 1\% move. The convention (dealers long calls, short puts)
mis-signs \HoldGammaWrong{} of it and, in a \HoldProfileDrop{} fall, reads
\HoldProfileDropConvention{} where the disclosed sides give \HoldProfileDropDealer{}.
At SPY's scale the correction is small: SPY's GEX moves from \GexConventionSpot{} to
\GexSignedSpot{} at spot. One issuer, one date.

\noindent\textbf{A bounded null.} In a test fixed in advance, netted FLEX dollar gamma
does not predict next-day single-stock volatility on average (minimum detectable effect
\GPredMDEShare{} of mean $|r|$ per standard deviation). A one-sided book hedged in the
stock would produce at least \GCalRatio{} times that under the square-root law of
impact, so such books are ruled out.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{How it was built.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Collectors for six public sources, with each OCC file reconciled against its own
  class totals and filed under the activity date it states.
\item Every number, table and figure is generated from result files; CI fails on any
  drift, including a figure drawn from stale results.
\item Each measurement and test was specified in a dated log entry before its figures,
  with amendments when something changed after; the paper went through simulated
  referee rounds whose findings changed headline numbers.
\item What went wrong is kept: a strike heuristic for finding FLEX, killed by ground truth
  (it misses \FlexOnGridShare{} of FLEX series), and a positive control that failed.
\end{itemize}
