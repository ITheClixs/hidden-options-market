\par\smallskip{\footnotesize Every FLEX series with open interest on 22 October 2026, assigned to one category within its root and expiry, in the order of the rows. A box is two strikes that each carry equal call and put open interest, with one count at both. A stock-leg call is an unpaired call struck at no more than 5\% of its mark plus its strike. A package leg is any other series with the count of a stock-leg call at its root and expiry. A same-strike pair is a strike with equal call and put open interest that is in neither. Open interest and value share are of the date's totals; mean value share averages the 475 report dates. Gross gamma is each category's share of gross FLEX dollar gamma on the 48 settlements and underlyings of Table~\ref{tab:gamma}.\par}
